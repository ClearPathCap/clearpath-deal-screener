Field Guide

Updated May 2026 · Charlotte · Lake Murray · STR markets

Market Update — May 2026

Rates have eased into the mid-6s after the 2024–2025 highs. Charlotte continues to outperform but the 12–18% YoY price spikes of 2022–2023 have normalized to ~5–7%. STR markets cooled meaningfully through 2024 as supply caught up with demand — Pigeon Forge especially saw 8–15% revenue declines per listing. Insurance in coastal SC is up 25–40% over 2023. Always verify current numbers in AirDNA, Zillow, and recent local comps before pulling the trigger.

Your Edge

Your full buildout and construction background is a serious advantage in fix & flips. You can identify structural issues others miss, scope repairs accurately, and self-perform work — giving you 20–35% cost savings over investors who hire everything out.

Strong flip markets in the region

Within your 1-hour radius. Lower price points than central Charlotte = more deals, higher % returns.
Lake Murray, SC outlook
Median ~$340k · Lakefront commands 50–80% premium
Strong second-home and retiree demand from Columbia, Charlotte, and Atlanta. Lakefront flips that nail the dock + view package consistently move fast. Inland fixers can be picked up at solid discounts.
Target buy price (70% rule)
Max Offer = (ARV × 0.70) − Repairs
Industry standard. If you're self-performing, you can push to 75–78% and still hit your number.
Average days on market (2026)
Flips: 30–75 days · Plan 5–6 month hold
Down from the 90+ day stretches of late 2023. Quality flips priced right are moving again.
Typical sell-side costs
Agent commission: 4.5–5.5% · Closing: 1–2%
Post-NAR settlement, buyer-agent commissions are negotiable and often paid by the buyer. Many sellers now pay 2–3% listing only. Use 5–6% conservatively, but ask your agent.

How to Estimate ARV

Step 1 — Find comps
Search Zillow/Redfin for sold homes within 0.5 miles, same bed/bath count, sold in last 90 days. Fully updated condition only.
Step 2 — Price per sq ft
Take 3–5 comps, calculate $/sqft for each. Average them. Multiply by your subject property's sqft.
Step 3 — Adjust conservatively
Subtract 5–10% from your calculated ARV. New investors consistently overshoot ARV — be conservative and protect your margin.

STR Market Benchmarks (2026)

Ocean Lakes SC (Myrtle Beach area)
Gross: $40k–$85k/yr · Occ: 58–70%
Slightly softer than 2022–2023 peak. Seasonal peak May–Sept. Golf cart community, very family-focused. Insurance up 30%+ since 2023 — factor in. HOA rules matter — verify STR is allowed.
Gatlinburg TN
Gross: $48k–$140k/yr · Occ: 62–75%
Year-round demand. Cabin/chalet style commands premium. Hot tub = 20–30% revenue increase. Stable through the STR correction — strong long-term play.
Pigeon Forge TN
Gross: $42k–$110k/yr · Occ: 60–70%
Took the hardest hit in the 2024 STR correction (oversupply). Prices off ~10–15% from peak which creates entry opportunities — but underwrite conservatively on revenue.
Sevierville TN
Gross: $38k–$95k/yr · Occ: 58–68%
Lower price points than Gatlinburg = higher CoC potential. 15 min to Dollywood. Strong value play.
Lake Murray SC
Gross: $35k–$80k/yr · Occ: 50–62%
Emerging STR market. Weekender/family lake retreats. Boat dock + lake view = 30–50% revenue lift. Lower nightly rates than mountain markets but cheaper entry and lower competition.
Your target CoC return
6% minimum · 10%+ = strong deal
In these markets, well-selected STRs regularly hit 10–15% CoC in 2026 — well above long-term rental returns.
