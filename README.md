# Deal Screener — by Clear Path Capital

A mobile-first deal underwriting tool for real estate investors. Analyze fix-and-flip and short-term rental deals in seconds, save your pipeline, share with partners, and submit qualifying deals to Clear Path Capital for funding review.

**Live site:** _coming soon_
**Capital partner:** [Clear Path Capital](https://clearpathcapfunding.com)

---

## What it does

- **Fix & Flip Analyzer** — Enter asking price, ARV, repair costs. Get profit, ROI, LTV, and a max-offer recommendation. Built-in repair cost estimator with self-perform toggle.
- **STR Analyzer** — Underwrite short-term rental deals across Ocean Lakes SC, Gatlinburg, Pigeon Forge, Lake Murray, or any custom market. Cash-on-cash, cap rate, cash flow, GRM.
- **Pipeline** — Save deals to your personal pipeline. Expand any card to see full details. Filter by type or "Hot Only."
- **Field Guide** — Market benchmarks, repair cost ranges, ARV estimation walkthrough, and STR market data updated for 2026.
- **Install as App** — Add to your home screen on iOS or Android for full-screen, app-like access.
- **Share** — Send the app to a friend or share a specific deal summary via SMS, WhatsApp, email, or your phone's native share sheet.

## Privacy

All deal data is stored locally on your device. Nothing is sent to any server. Deleting browser data or uninstalling the app removes your pipeline.

## Tech

Vanilla HTML, CSS, JavaScript. No frameworks, no build step. Hosted on GitHub Pages. PWA-installable.
