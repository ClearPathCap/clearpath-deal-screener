DealFit Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ClearPath Capital, LLC (“Clear Path Capital,” “CPC,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use DealFit.

DealFit is a real-estate deal analysis service operated by ClearPath Capital, LLC.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create or access a DealFit account, we process information such as your:

  • email address;
  • authentication information; and
  • account identifier.

DealFit currently uses passwordless email authentication.

Deal and Property Information

When you use DealFit, you may provide and save information about real-estate opportunities, including:

  • property addresses;
  • purchase prices;
  • rehabilitation budgets;
  • after-repair values;
  • rents;
  • taxes;
  • insurance;
  • association expenses;
  • financing assumptions;
  • loan information; and
  • other deal inputs.

DealFit may calculate and store resulting metrics such as DSCR, cash flow, cash-on-cash return, leverage, verdicts, and related analysis.

Market and Product Preferences

We may process information about:

  • markets you select;
  • onboarding status;
  • interface preferences;
  • plan or tier information; and
  • saved pipeline activity.

Some interface preferences are stored locally in your browser.

Subscription and Entitlement Information

For paid or complimentary access, we may process:

  • your DealFit user identifier;
  • your Stripe customer identifier;
  • subscription status;
  • subscription period information;
  • payment-success or payment-failure status;
  • entitlement tier;
  • promotional or complimentary-access status;
  • redemption records; and
  • related transaction-processing records.

Payment-card information is entered on Stripe-hosted payment surfaces. DealFit does not need to receive or store your full payment-card number to provide subscription access.

Technical Information

When you access DealFit, ordinary web and infrastructure services may automatically receive technical information such as:

  • IP address;
  • browser or device information;
  • request information; and
  • timestamps.

DealFit also uses browser-local storage for certain account or interface state.

As of the Effective Date, the shipped DealFit application does not include an advertising pixel, tag manager, or dedicated analytics SDK.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • authenticate users;
  • provide DealFit;
  • save and retrieve deals;
  • calculate and display deal analysis;
  • manage selected markets and pipeline information;
  • determine account tier and feature access;
  • process subscription and complimentary access;
  • prevent duplicate or unauthorized subscription activity;
  • provide sharing and export features;
  • route user-requested funding information to Clear Path Capital;
  • maintain security and reliability;
  • diagnose errors;
  • prevent fraud or abuse;
  • provide customer support;
  • comply with legal obligations; and
  • operate and improve DealFit.

3. Payments

DealFit uses Stripe for paid subscriptions, Checkout, payment processing, and the Customer Portal.

When you purchase a subscription, payment information is provided directly to Stripe.

We receive information from Stripe that is needed to identify your customer or subscription, determine payment and subscription status, manage your DealFit entitlement, and respond to billing events.

Stripe processes payment information under its own privacy practices.

4. Authentication and Data Infrastructure

DealFit uses Supabase for services that include authentication, database storage, and server-side application functions.

Your account information, saved pipeline data, selected markets, entitlement information, and related application records may therefore be processed through Supabase infrastructure.

5. Hosting and Software Delivery

DealFit's static web application is hosted through GitHub Pages.

DealFit also loads certain software or presentation resources from third-party services, including:

  • Google Fonts; and
  • esm.sh, which is used to deliver a pinned software dependency.

When your browser requests resources from these services, those providers may receive ordinary network information such as your IP address, browser details, and request metadata.

Their handling of information is governed by their own privacy practices.

6. Clear Path Capital Funding Requests

DealFit includes an optional path for users who want to explore financing through Clear Path Capital.

If you choose to use that feature, DealFit may transfer selected deal information to Clear Path Capital's funding website, including relevant financial or property-analysis inputs.

This transfer occurs because you request the funding handoff.

Information submitted through the Clear Path Capital funding process may then be governed by additional disclosures or terms applicable to that brokerage or funding process.

You do not need to request funding in order to use DealFit's general analysis features.

7. Sharing and Export You Initiate

DealFit may let you copy or share deal information using features such as:

  • your device's native sharing interface;
  • your clipboard;
  • messaging applications; or
  • third-party destinations you select.

These transfers occur when you initiate them.

Once information is shared with another person or service, that recipient's privacy practices may apply.

8. When We Share Information

We may disclose information:

To Service Providers

We use service providers to operate DealFit, including providers used for:

  • authentication and database infrastructure;
  • payment and subscription processing;
  • website hosting;
  • software or font delivery; and
  • other technical operations.

These providers receive information as needed to perform their services.

At Your Direction

We share information when you direct DealFit to:

  • share or export a deal;
  • send information to another destination; or
  • initiate a Clear Path Capital funding handoff.

For Legal, Security, or Business Reasons

We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to:

  • comply with law, legal process, or valid government requests;
  • protect DealFit, Clear Path Capital, users, or others;
  • investigate fraud, abuse, security incidents, or violations;
  • enforce applicable agreements; or
  • support a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, sale of assets, or similar business transaction, subject to applicable law.

9. Information We Do Not Sell for Advertising

As of the Effective Date, DealFit does not include advertising technology or a dedicated advertising or analytics tracking SDK.

We do not use the deal information you save in DealFit to sell targeted advertising to you.

If our practices materially change, we will update this Privacy Policy as appropriate.

10. Browser Storage

DealFit uses local browser storage for certain interface and account-related state.

Examples may include:

  • displayed tier state;
  • onboarding status;
  • market-selection state;
  • interface preferences; and
  • install or usage hints.

Clearing browser storage may reset some local settings but does not necessarily delete information associated with your server-side DealFit account.

11. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to:

  • provide DealFit;
  • maintain your account and saved information;
  • administer subscriptions and entitlements;
  • maintain transaction and security records;
  • resolve disputes;
  • comply with legal obligations; and
  • support legitimate business and operational needs.

Different categories of information may have different retention periods.

When information is no longer reasonably necessary, we may delete, anonymize, or otherwise dispose of it, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

12. Security

We use administrative and technical measures designed to protect DealFit information.

No internet service or storage system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

You should maintain control of the email account used to sign in to DealFit and contact us if you believe your DealFit access has been compromised.

13. Your Choices and Privacy Rights

You can choose:

  • whether to save particular deal information;
  • whether to share or export a deal;
  • whether to request Clear Path Capital funding; and
  • whether to maintain a paid subscription.

Depending on where you live and applicable law, you may also have rights regarding personal information, such as rights to request access, correction, deletion, or other treatment of certain information.

To make a privacy request, contact:

support@clearpathcapfunding.com

We may need to verify your identity before completing a request. Certain information may be retained where permitted or required by law.

14. Children's Privacy

DealFit is designed for real-estate investment and business-purpose use and is not directed to children under 13.

We do not knowingly seek personal information from children under 13 through DealFit.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to DealFit, contact us.

15. Third-Party Websites and Services

DealFit may link to or interact with third-party websites or services.

This Privacy Policy governs Clear Path Capital's handling of information through DealFit. It does not control the independent privacy practices of third parties.

We encourage you to review the privacy information provided by services you choose to use.

16. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as DealFit, our service providers, or legal requirements change.

The current version will display its effective date.

If a change materially affects how we handle personal information, we will provide notice as reasonably appropriate.

17. Contact Us

Questions or requests concerning this Privacy Policy or DealFit's handling of information may be sent to:

DealFit Support
support@clearpathcapfunding.com

DealFit is operated by ClearPath Capital, LLC.
