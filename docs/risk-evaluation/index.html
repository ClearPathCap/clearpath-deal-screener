How the risk evaluation works

Every analysis clears three bars before DealFit calls it strong: a dollar floor, a return or debt-coverage bar, and a stress test. The stress test takes the deal you entered, applies the same conservative haircuts to every deal, and recalculates it with the same engine. The haircuts are fixed — you cannot loosen them to make a deal look better.

Reading the margin of safety

  • Strong — the deal clears the stress test with room to spare. Fix & flip: stressed profit is at least half of your profit target (or $25,000 when no target is set). Rental: stressed DSCR is at least 1.15 on 1–4 units or 1.20 on 5–8 units. BRRRR: the same DSCR bar, with no more than 25% of your cash left in after the refinance.
  • Tight — the deal survives the stress test, but with little cushion. Small surprises in value, rehab or rent could erase it.
  • Fails — a modest downside turns the deal negative. DealFit will not grade a deal that fails the stress test as a strong deal, however good the base case looks.

The verdict then turns the numbers into a next step — for example a counter price and a walk-away price on a flip, or a note that rent does not cover the debt on a rental.

What it does not do

  • It does not verify your inputs. After-repair value, rehab cost and rent come from you or from market presets — confirm them with comps, contractor bids and leases.
  • It does not model every risk: title, permits, insurance availability, market shifts and timeline delays beyond one month are outside the test.
  • It is not an appraisal, a loan offer or financial, tax or legal advice. Every figure is an estimate.

Questions

What does the DealFit stress test change?

It re-runs the same deal with fixed, conservative haircuts. Fix and flip: after-repair value down 5%, rehab up 10% and one extra month of holding. Long-term rental: rent down 5%, vacancy up 3 points and the interest rate up 0.5%. BRRRR: after-repair value down 5% and rent down 5%. On 5 to 8 unit properties, vacancy is also floored at one fully vacant unit.

Can I change the stress-test haircuts?

No. The haircuts and risk floors are fixed constants, so every deal is held to the same test. You can change the deal's own inputs and targets, such as price, rehab budget, rent and your profit or cash-on-cash target.

Does a Strong margin of safety mean the deal is safe?

No. It means the numbers you entered still clear DealFit's bars after a modest downside. The result is only as good as the inputs, and it is a screening estimate, not an appraisal, a loan offer or financial advice. Verify value, rehab cost and rent independently before you commit.

Does the short-term rental analyzer run a stress test?

Not today. The short-term rental analyzer grades the deal on cash-on-cash return against your target and on cap rate. To test the downside, lower the occupancy rate or the revenue estimate yourself and analyze again.

Is DealFit free?

Yes. The analyzers and the stress test are free to use, and no account is needed to run an analysis. A free account saves deals to a pipeline. Paid plans add market data for the markets you invest in; they never change access to financing from Clear Path Capital.
