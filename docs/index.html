How to Estimate ARV

Step 1 — Find comps
Search Zillow/Redfin for sold homes within 0.5 miles, same bed/bath count, sold in last 90 days. Fully updated condition only.
Step 2 — Price per sq ft
Take 3–5 comps, calculate $/sqft for each. Average them. Multiply by your subject property's sqft.
Step 3 — Adjust conservatively
Subtract 5–10% from your calculated ARV. New investors consistently overshoot ARV — be conservative and protect your margin.
The 70% Rule
Max Offer = (ARV × 0.70) − Repairs
Industry standard for fix & flips. If you're self-performing, you can push to 75–78% and still hit your target.

Key Formulas & Definitions

ARV — After Repair Value
What the property will sell for after all repairs and updates are complete. This is your exit number — everything else is calculated from it.
MAO — Maximum Allowable Offer
(ARV × 0.70) − Repairs
The highest price you can pay and still hit your profit target. Never offer more than your MAO.
LTV — Loan to Value
Loan Amount ÷ ARV
Most private lenders want LTV under 70% of ARV. The Deal Screener calculates this automatically.
ROI — Return on Investment
Net Profit ÷ Total All-In Cost
Measures your return relative to total dollars invested. Target 20%+ for a strong flip.
CoC — Cash-on-Cash Return (STR)
Annual Cash Flow ÷ Total Cash Invested
The annual return on the actual cash you put in (down payment + furnishing). 6%+ is a baseline target for STR.
Cap Rate
NOI ÷ Purchase Price
Net Operating Income divided by purchase price — measures return as if you paid cash. 6%+ is solid for STR.
NOI — Net Operating Income
Effective Rent − Operating Expenses
Revenue after all operating expenses (platform fees, taxes, maintenance, management) but before debt service.
GRM — Gross Rent Multiplier
Purchase Price ÷ Gross Annual Rent
Quick valuation check. Lower is better — under 10x is strong, 10–15x is moderate, above 15x may be overpriced.

Private Money Lending Basics

Key metrics lenders care about
LTV · ARV · Borrower experience · Exit strategy
Most private lenders want LTV under 70% of ARV. Your deal screener confirms this before you present a deal.
What Clear Path Capital does
CPC brokers private money — meaning they work on your behalf across dozens of lenders to find the best terms. You don't call lenders; they do. That's the fiduciary difference.
Build your track record
As you complete flips, document everything — before/afters, timelines, profit. That becomes your credibility when raising private capital or applying for larger loans.
Typical private money terms
Rate: 8–12% · LTV: up to 70% ARV · Term: 6–18 mo
Terms vary widely by borrower experience, market, and deal quality. CPC can often improve on rates you'd get going direct.
