Questions

What revenue figure should I enter?

Enter the potential annual revenue at 100% occupancy and your expected occupancy rate. If your revenue estimate already includes occupancy, as many market-data tools do, enter it and set occupancy to 100%.

How does DealFit grade a short-term rental?

On cash-on-cash return against your target and on cap rate. The strongest verdict needs cash-on-cash at or above your target and a cap rate of at least 6%. DealFit also shows DSCR — net operating income divided by the loan payment.

Does the short-term rental calculator include a stress test?

No. Unlike the fix and flip, long-term rental and BRRRR analyzers, it has no built-in stress test today. To test the downside, lower the occupancy rate or the revenue estimate and analyze again.
