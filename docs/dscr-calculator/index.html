Questions

How does DealFit calculate DSCR?

DSCR is net operating income divided by annual principal-and-interest debt service. Net operating income is rent after vacancy, minus property management, maintenance, property taxes, insurance, HOA and owner-paid utilities. The CapEx reserve is subtracted afterwards for cash flow, not inside DSCR.

Will a lender calculate the same DSCR?

Not necessarily. Lenders and loan programs calculate DSCR in different ways, for example on gross rent against the full monthly payment including taxes and insurance. Treat DealFit's DSCR as a screening estimate.

What DSCR does DealFit treat as strong?

DealFit's strongest rental verdict needs a DSCR of at least 1.25, at least $6,000 a year of cash flow or a cash-on-cash return at or above your target, positive monthly cash flow, and a deal that survives the stress test. A DSCR below 1.0 means rent does not cover the debt at that price and down payment.

What does the rental stress test change?

On 1 to 4 units it lowers rent by 5%, adds 3 points of vacancy and raises the interest rate by 0.5 percentage points. On 5 to 8 units it uses the same rent and rate haircuts and the higher of 5 extra points of vacancy or one fully vacant unit.
