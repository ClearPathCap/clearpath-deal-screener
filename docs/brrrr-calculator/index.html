The BRRRR stress test

DealFit re-runs the deal with ARV −5% (so the refinance loan is smaller) and rent −5%; on 5–8 units, vacancy also rises by 5 points or to one fully vacant unit, whichever is higher.

Margin of safetyMeaning
StrongStressed DSCR of at least 1.15 (1.20 on 5–8 units) and no more than 25% of your cash left in
TightSurvives the stress test, with less cushion
FailsStressed DSCR below 1.0, or more than 50% of your cash left in the deal

Questions

How does DealFit calculate cash out at refinance?

The new loan is the after-repair value times the refinance LTV you enter. Cash out is that loan minus the payoff of the acquisition (bridge) loan and the refinance costs. Capital left in the deal is the cash you invested minus the cash out.

What does the BRRRR stress test change?

It lowers the after-repair value by 5%, which shrinks the refinance loan, and lowers rent by 5%. On 5 to 8 units, vacancy also rises by 5 points or to one fully vacant unit, whichever is higher. The margin of safety is Fails if the stressed DSCR is below 1.0 or more than 50% of your cash stays in the deal, and Strong when the stressed DSCR is at least 1.15 (1.20 on 5 to 8 units) with no more than 25% left in.

When does DealFit say a BRRRR breaks?

When the refinance would not pay off the acquisition loan, when the all-in cost meets or exceeds the after-repair value so no equity is created, or when rent does not cover the refinance debt.
